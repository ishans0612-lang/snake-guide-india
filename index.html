First aid information

Suspected snakebite? Get medical care urgently.

Do not wait for symptoms or try to identify the snake. Move away from it and contact local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital as quickly and safely as possible. This guide does not provide an emergency phone number.

01 — Move away and stay calm

Keep the person still and reassured. Limit walking and movement; do not approach or try to catch the snake.

02 — Keep the limb still

Remove rings, watches, or tight clothing near the bite before swelling. Arrange urgent transport for medical assessment.

03 — Note details only safely

If it can be done without going closer, remember the time and general appearance. Do not delay care to take a photo.

Do not: cut or suck the wound, apply ice, use a tourniquet, attempt home remedies, or delay hospital treatment. Follow instructions from local medical professionals; first-aid protocols can vary by region.
