SNAKEBITE FIRST AID

Suspected snakebite? Seek urgent medical care.

Move away from the snake and contact local emergency services or get to the nearest hospital urgently. Do not wait for symptoms, and do not delay care to identify or photograph the snake. This guide intentionally does not list an emergency phone number.

01 — Keep still

Reassure the person, limit movement, and keep the bitten limb as still as practical.

02 — Get medical help

Arrange urgent transport to a medical facility. Remove rings, watches, or tight items near the bite before swelling.

03 — Leave the snake alone

Do not approach, catch, or kill it. Note details only from a safe distance and only if this does not delay care.

Do not cut or suck the wound, apply ice, use a tourniquet, try home remedies, or delay hospital treatment. Follow local medical professionals' advice; protocols can vary by region.
