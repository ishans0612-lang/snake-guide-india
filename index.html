1. Move to safety

Keep away from the snake. Do not try to catch or kill it.

2. Call for help

Contact local emergency services and arrange urgent medical care.

3. Stay still

Keep the person calm and limit movement while help is coming.

4. Avoid harmful remedies

Do not cut or suck the wound, apply ice, or use a tight tourniquet.
