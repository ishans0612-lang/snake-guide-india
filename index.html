IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

Snakebite? Get medical help immediately.

A suspected snakebite is an emergency, even if there is no immediate pain.

Call Emergency Services: 112

01 — Stay still

Reassure the person, limit movement and keep the bitten limb still.

02 — Go to hospital

Arrange urgent transport. Remove rings or tight items before swelling develops.

03 — Keep away

Do not approach, catch or kill the snake. Remember its appearance only from a safe distance.

Never cut or suck the wound, apply ice, use a tourniquet or delay hospital treatment for home remedies.
