Load up to 2,000 snake species records from the public iNaturalist taxonomy, then browse freely licensed photo matches from Wikimedia Commons.

Important: This catalog is educational only. Taxonomy records do not confirm a snake’s identity, range, or danger. Venom status is intentionally not guessed. Never use a photo or this catalog to identify a snake or decide whether it is safe.
Press “Load” to fetch the public species list. No account or paid API required.

Species names are retrieved from the public iNaturalist taxonomy API and may change with taxonomy updates. The list is capped at 2,000 records to keep this lightweight static site responsive. Photos are searched through Wikimedia Commons only; image cards show contributor and license links when available. Photos are illustrative, not identification evidence. Free APIs and internet access are required to load the catalog and photos.
