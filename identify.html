Safety first: Do not approach, touch, trap, or provoke a snake to take a photo. Never use these suggestions to decide whether it is venomous or safe. Results can be wrong. If someone may have been bitten, move away and contact local emergency services and a medical professional immediately.

To produce suggestions, the selected image is sent to the public iNaturalist API. Do not select an image containing private or sensitive information. This page does not store your image. Camera access generally requires HTTPS or localhost.
